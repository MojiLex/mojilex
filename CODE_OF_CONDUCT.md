# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in MojiLex a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, responsibility for
mistakes, focus on what is best for the community, and respect for differing
viewpoints. Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention,
trolling or insulting comments, harassment, publication of others' private
information, and conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers clarify and enforce these standards and may remove, edit, or
reject comments, commits, code, data, issues, and other contributions that are
not aligned with them. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and should
protect reporter privacy.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents
MojiLex in public spaces.

## Enforcement

For non-sensitive conduct concerns, contact the maintainers through a GitHub
issue. Sensitive reports should use the private advisory channel documented in
`SECURITY.md`. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and avoid
retaliation.

Consequences may range from a private correction or warning, through temporary
restriction, to permanent removal from project interaction for sustained or
severe violations. An appeal is reviewed by a different maintainer when one is
available, following `GOVERNANCE.md`.
