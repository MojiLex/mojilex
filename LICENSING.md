# Licensing boundaries

This repository contains different categories of material.

## CC0-1.0

The following MojiLex-created material is dedicated to the public domain under
CC0-1.0, as stated in `LICENSE`:

- canonical metadata under `data/`;
- descriptions, usage phrases, semantic tags, and content labels;
- anonymized tombstones; and
- textual example metadata and normative data vectors under `examples/`.

CC0 applies only to rights the contributor or MojiLex can grant.

## MIT

Maintenance scripts, tests, JSON Schema, GitHub workflow files, and repository
documentation are licensed under MIT, as stated in `LICENSE-CODE`.

## Rights not granted

MojiLex stores platform IDs and factual metadata but does not redistribute
third-party emoji media. Neither CC0 nor MIT grants rights in third-party emoji
artwork, animation, collection names, characters, brands, trademarks, publicity
rights, or other material owned by third parties. Those rights remain with their
respective owners.

No original emoji, preview, frame, contact sheet, or media-derived binary may be
committed. A future media-storage feature would require separate permission and
policy and is outside schema v1.
